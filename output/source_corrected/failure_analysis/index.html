Motion control: 80 summaries of adjacent-frame differences and optical flow on a 2x2 grid, plus train-standardized logistic regression. It uses the same 32 time anchors as poses, with neighboring decoded frames for valid flow. Quality-only control: seven detection-count/quality descriptors and a fixed-C linear classifier, with no joint positions or motion.

These controls differ in architecture and feature count from the TCN. Motion examines adjacent pixels around each anchor rather than only the sampled pose frame. They diagnose useful signals and confounds; they are not published deep RGB/flow benchmarks.

No exact or prefix-compatible source-ID overlaps remain across this split. Other related footage may escape filename and image-hash checks; manual source review remains necessary.

Measured failures

The combined model has 16 errors: 6 false positives and 10 false negatives. 11 errors occur above the 25% pose-coverage cutoff. 13 clips are wrong in all four skeleton variants. Low pose coverage does not explain every failure.

Short tracks and discarded detections indicate possible information loss, but these numbers cannot identify actual identity switches. Labels remain the dataset labels until full-video review.
