# Source-overlap review: 7 November 2026

All 245 clips were fully decoded (22,027 frames). Sampling near 4 FPS produced 3,638 frames; 29,856 pairs from different filename-derived source groups were compared.

The heuristic flagged 5 pairs. Visual review of 20 candidate sheets supports grouping 13 pairs. Two links are redundant through existing groups, so the known source-group count falls from 215 to 204.

The five automatic flags alone would miss several cropped or watermarked recordings. Grouping records shared recording sources, including different segments. Labels were preserved. Full decoding plus sampled source evidence does not certify every label, every pose, or absence of other shared footage.
